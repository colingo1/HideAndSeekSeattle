Rules

General

  • Each team will have one chance to hide, and one chance to seek. Whichever team has the longer hiding time wins!
  • We'll be playing a Small game, any references on cards use the "S" number.
  • The first team will start from Westlake Center at 10:00 AM, and have 30 minutes to hide. The second team will start from the previous team's hiding place, and have a slightly longer 40 minutes to account for less transit availability.
  • All hiders should have their locations off, and seekers should have their locations on.
  • Hiding and Seeking teams are both free to use any form of public transportation, not only the lines listed on the map. This includes buses, trains, streetcars, and others, but not things like Ubers or Lime Scooters.
  • Both teams are free to do any sort of research they want during the game, with the exceptions of
    • Google Street View
    • Reverse image searches
    • LLMs
  • Your team must stay (functionally) together; you can't split up into different units.

Hiders

Hiding Locations

  • When your hiding time is finished, you must be within a radius of one of the allowed Transit Stops. If you are in the radius of multiple, you must select one.
    This stop is now your Hiding Zone. You're free to explore your zone, but cannot leave it for the remainder of your run.
  • When the opposing team enters your Hiding Zone, you must stop where you are, and remain in that location for the rest of the game. Pay attention to the Seekers' locations, so you ensure you're in your spot.
  • Your final hiding spot must be publicly accessible, and within 10 feet of a public pathway or street.

Answering Questions

  • When the Seekers ask you a question, you have 5 minutes (or 10 minutes for a photo) to answer it. You'll probably want to use this full time, to stall the seekers a bit more. You are also free to move around your area in this time period, which can impact the answer to your question.
  • If you can't answer a question because you're immobile in your final hiding spot, you must tell the Seekers.
  • After answering a question, draw cards from the deck based on the question type.

Cards

  • You can hold up to 6 cards in your hand at a time. You can temporarily have more, but must play or discard to get back to 6.
  • The hiders have 3 types of cards available:
    • Time Bonuses: These cards can't be played. At the end of the game, the total of your time bonuses is added to your run length.
    • Curses: These cards allow you to negatively impact the Seekers. To use one, pay its Casting Cost shown on the card, then text a photo of it to the Seekers.
    • Powerups: These cards allow you to manipulate your hand or interact with Seeker questions.
  • You can play a curse at any time, but cannot have more than one curse that prevents seekers from asking questions or taking transit active at a time.
  • Randomize Question: This card can be sent in response to a question (after the allowed time), and forces the seekers to select a new random question from the same category. You have another time window to answer the second question.

Seekers

Questions

  • To ask a question, text it to the hiders, along with what cards it's worth. If it's a location-based question, send them a pin of your location or what your nearest X is.
  • You can only have one question active at a time.
  • You may ask the same question a second time, but asking a question a second time doubles its price. (then triples, etc.)

Available Questions/Clarifications

  • Matching Questions (Draw 3, Pick 1):
    • I'm on X transit line. Does my line stop at your station? (Must be on a transit line and moving.)
    • My nearest transit station is X. Does it have the same number of letters as your transit station?
    • My nearest street is X. Is it your nearest street?
    • (Level 1 administrative district) I'm in X City Council district. Are you in the same district?
    • (Level 2 administrative district) I'm in X Zip Code. Are you in the same Zip Code?
    • I'm on X landmass. Are you on the same landmass? For the purposes of this question, Seattle's landmasses are North, South, and West, divided by the waterways.
    • My nearest park is X. Is it your nearest park?
    • My nearest golf course is X. Is it your nearest golf course? By our definition, the golf courses in Seattle are: Jackson Park, Interbay, Broadmoor, Jefferson Park, West Seattle, Sand Point, and Green Lake.
    • My nearest museum is X. Is it your nearest museum?
    • My nearest movie theater is X. Is it your nearest movie theater?
    • My nearest hospital is X. Is it your nearest hospital?
    • My nearest library is X. Is it your nearest library?
  • Measuring Questions (Draw 3, Pick 1):
    • I'm X distance from a rail station. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a body of water. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a coastline. Are you closer or further? A coastline here refers to any part of the Puget Sound, until the locks.
    • I'm X distance from a park. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a (Woodland Park) Zoo. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a (Seattle) Aquarium. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a golf course. Are you closer or further? See previous golf course definition.
    • I'm X distance from a museum. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a movie theater. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a hospital. Are you closer or further?
    • I'm X distance from a library. Are you closer or further?
  • Thermometers (Draw 2, Pick 1):
    • Before all thermometers, announce that you're starting a thermometer, and send a pin of your starting location. Then, after traveling the specified distance, send them a second pin, and ask if you're now closer or further.
    • I've just traveled at least 1/2 mile. Am I closer or further from you?
    • I've just traveled at least 3 miles. Am I closer or further from you?
  • Radar (Draw 2, Pick 1):
    • For all radars, send a pin of your current location.
    • Are you within 1/4 mile of me?
    • Are you within 1/2 mile of me?
    • Are you within 1 mile of me?
    • Are you within 3 miles of me?
    • Are you within 5 miles of me?
    • Are you within 10 miles of me?
    • Are you within 10 miles of me?
    • Are you within 20 miles of me?
    • Are you within X miles of me? (Seeker's choice)
  • Photos (Draw 1, 10 minute time limit):
    • Send me a photo of a tree. Must include the entire tree.
    • Send me a photo of the sky. Place your phone on the ground, shoot directly up.
    • Send me a photo of you. Selfie mode, Arm parallel to the ground, fully extended.
    • Send me a photo of the widest street you can see. Must include both sides of the street.
    • Send me a photo of the tallest structure in your sightline (from your perspective). Must include the top and both sides. The top must be in the top 1/3 of the frame.
    • Send me a photo of any building visible from your transit stop. Must stand directly outside a transit station entrance. Must include roof, both sides, with teh top of building in top 1/3 of the frame.
